## Overview

Periodic Keyboard is a self-initiated 3D experiment exploring the idea of combining two familiar systems: a computer keyboard and the periodic table of elements. The concept asks a simple question: what would a keyboard look like if every key represented a chemical element?

## Contribution

I developed and modeled the concept entirely in Blender, translating the structure and visual language of the periodic table into a functional keyboard form. Each key represents an individual element, turning the keyboard into a three-dimensional interpretation of the periodic table.

The project allowed me to explore the intersection of digital interfaces, physical objects, and scientific systems through 3D modeling and visual experimentation.
