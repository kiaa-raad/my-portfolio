## Overview

3D Threads Logo is a self-initiated exploration of the Threads logo through a three-dimensional approach. The project investigates how an existing digital brand mark can be reinterpreted as a physical-looking 3D object while incorporating elements of Instagram's visual language.

## Contribution

I recreated and experimented with the Threads logo in Blender, transforming the flat mark into a three-dimensional form. The 3D treatment allowed me to explore Instagram's brand DNA through its visual language, materials, lighting, and color palette, creating a new interpretation of the logo beyond its original two-dimensional form.
