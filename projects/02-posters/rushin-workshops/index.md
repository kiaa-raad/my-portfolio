## Overview

Rushin Workshops is a series of educational and scientific workshops designed for students, covering different subjects and hands-on learning experiences. Each workshop introduces participants to a specific scientific field through practical activities, experiments, and exploration.

## Contribution

I developed the poster series for the Rushin workshops using AI-generated photography and photomanipulation as the main visual approaches. For each workshop, I extracted distinctive elements from its scientific activities and combined them into unexpected compositions to create curious and engaging images that encourage students to explore the workshop.

Alongside the visual development, I designed a workflow that could turn workshop information into a ready-to-publish poster through a structured process. This approach allowed the posters to move efficiently from workshop brief to final assets ready for digital publishing and print.
