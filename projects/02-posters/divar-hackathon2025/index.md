## Overview

Divar Hackathon 2025 was an event organized by Divar, bringing together participants to collaborate, experiment, and develop solutions around real-world challenges. The main theme of this edition was security, with participants working intensively throughout the hackathon to explore ideas and build practical solutions.

## Contribution

I was responsible for developing the visual identity of the event. The concept was built around the line, an important visual element in Divar's identity, reimagined as a tangled thread representing a complex problem.

Throughout the visual system, the thread gradually becomes untangled and transforms into a shield, symbolizing the process of solving problems and the central theme of security. This visual element was extended across the event identity and used to design participant hoodies and other event applications, creating a cohesive system throughout the hackathon.
