## Overview

Divar Yalda is an internal celebration organized for Divar employees around Yalda Night, one of the most important Iranian cultural traditions. The event brings together the team to celebrate Yalda Night.

## Contribution

I combined traditional Yalda elements such as pomegranates, nuts, Hafez's poetry, and music with the existing visual language of Divar.

The goal was to create a visual that felt distinctly connected to Yalda while remaining consistent with Divar's brand identity. These cultural references were reinterpreted through Divar's visual system to create a cohesive and contemporary event graphic.
