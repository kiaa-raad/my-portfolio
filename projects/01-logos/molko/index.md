## Overview

Molko is an educational website focused on chemistry, created to make chemistry-related knowledge and learning resources more accessible through a digital platform.

## Contribution

The logo concept combines the Persian letter “م”, the first letter of the brand name, with the molecular structure of benzene.

The combination creates a visual connection between the brand name and its educational focus, using the familiar geometric structure of benzene as a foundation for a distinctive and conceptually relevant identity.
