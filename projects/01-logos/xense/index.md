## Overview

Xense is a Canadian company focused on the development and production of biosensors for the detection and measurement of target substances. Its technologies have applications across fields including medicine, agriculture, and industrial wastewater treatment, bringing sensing and measurement technologies into a range of scientific and practical contexts.

## Contribution

The identity brings together four core concepts connected to the company's name and field: the letter “X” as the first letter of Xense, light as the fundamental medium that allows us to see, the atom as the basic building block of chemistry, and the eye as the sensory organ associated with sight.

These elements were combined into a unified visual language that connects sensing, visibility, and the microscopic world of chemistry, creating a distinctive identity rooted in the scientific nature of the company.
