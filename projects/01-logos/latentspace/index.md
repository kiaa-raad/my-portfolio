## Overview
Latent Space is a technology-driven company specializing in advanced AI solutions, data visualization, and predictive analytics. The project focused on developing a visual identity that reflects the brand's approach to uncovering hidden insights, navigating complex datasets, and enabling new possibilities through technology.

## Contribution
The identity explores the relationship between exploration, location, and data, combining a cursive “L” with a location marker to create a distinctive and memorable visual symbol.

The design direction focused on balancing technological innovation with simplicity, precision, and elegance.
