## Overview

UpZone is an interior architecture and design platform that helps clients design, renovate, and transform their homes. The service provides interior design and renovation solutions through its website, helping users improve their living spaces with professional guidance.

## Contribution
The logo combines three core elements: the letter “U”, a simplified representation of a house, and an upward arrow.

The combination connects the brand name with its purpose, using the house to represent the client's living space and the arrow to communicate improvement, transformation, and moving a space to a new level. The resulting identity is designed to feel simple, contemporary, and relevant to the field of interior architecture.
