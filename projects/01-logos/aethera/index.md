## Overview

Aethera Pharmaceuticals is a pharmaceutical company focused on improving human health through innovation, integrity, and transparency. The brand aims to build trust through forward-thinking healthcare solutions while maintaining a strong commitment to patients and ethical practices.

## Contribution
The logo concept combines the familiar visual language of pharmaceutical capsules with a minimal approach to negative space.

The shape of pills is used to construct a bold, clean “A” within the negative space, directly connecting the symbol to Aethera's name and its focus on healthcare. The resulting identity balances simplicity and recognition while creating a distinctive visual mark rooted in the pharmaceutical context.
