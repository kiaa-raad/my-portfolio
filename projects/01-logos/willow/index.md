## Overview
Willow is a beauty brand dedicated to natural, plant-based skincare. The brand's ethos centers on the idea that true beauty comes from nature, with products crafted using sustainable botanical ingredients to nourish and enhance the skin.

The visual identity was developed to reflect Willow's natural, eco-conscious values while creating a sense of elegance, serenity, and refined beauty.

## Contribution
The core concept combines the essence of a flower with the brand's initial “W”, creating a distinctive symbol that connects the identity to its botanical foundation.

The design direction focused on creating a balance between organic forms and modern simplicity, resulting in an identity that feels natural, sophisticated, and suitable for a luxury skincare brand.
