## Overview

Archa is a construction company focused on building and developing urban spaces. The brand identity was developed to communicate ideas of progress, reliability, construction, and long-term growth.

## Contribution

The logo combines several symbolic elements into a unified mark: a hand representing the contractor and the act of building, an ascending line representing progress, an urban skyline representing construction and the city, and a column representing strength, support, and trust.

The letter “آ” serves as another fundamental element of the mark, directly representing Archa while integrating with the architectural language of the symbol. Together, these elements create an identity that connects the company's name with its values and field of work.
